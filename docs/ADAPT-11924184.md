## What the source is, in one paragraph

A 14.8 s two-shot excerpt of a deep-throating performance. Shot 1
(0–7.3 s): wide overhead framing, body geometry, partner's hands
planted on her back. One cut at 49%. Shot 2 (7.3–14.8 s): face
close-up, held to the end. The stroke cycle runs 0.67–0.93 s with
±30% humanised spacing, ≈ 17–19 cycles, depth effort peaking ≈ 70%
through the take, and the take stops mid-cycle — no climax, no
conclusion. The headline technique: **the face is phase-locked to
the stroke** — at the top of every cycle her eyes open half-lidded
and her gaze goes up into the camera; at the bottom her eyes close
and the face turns inward. Cheek fullness scales with depth.

## What carries over

- **The two-shot grammar, whole.** Redlight is the recorded register;
  this adaptation is a *recorded rite* built exactly on the source's
  edit: Shot A (wide) → one cut at the rite's midpoint → Shot B
  (face), held. No additional angles, no intra-shot camera motion.
- **The phase-locked face.** Her awareness of the lens is the rite's
  clock: gaze delivered to camera at the top of every cycle, eyes
  closed at the bottom. In Redlight's terms this is not flavour —
  it is the input surface (below).
- **The cycle as data.** Period nominal 24 steps (the recipe's
  0.67–0.93 s band expressed at the core's step rate), scripted
  per-cycle jitter of ±30% from a seeded table (deterministic core:
  the jitter is authored data, not runtime randomness), depth curve
  descent ≈ 45% / bottom dwell ≈ 15% / withdrawal ≈ 40%, depth
  effort envelope peaking at ≈ 70% of the rite's cycles.
- **The non-ending.** The rite stops mid-cycle on a gaze beat, like
  the source. There is no culmination event to wire; the arousal
  machinery is not touched.

## What changes for the style

The source is a film excerpt; Redlight is a *sampler the player
operates*. The camera's passivity becomes the player's job: the
player holds the audience side of the Lens Toy loop while the rite
runs its authored programme. Her gaze beats are addressed to the
player's lens — answering them is play.

## Mechanics mapping (onto Redlight's existing systems)

- **Lens Toy core mode — new programme "Deep Take".** A Lens Toy
  programme in the core mode's existing shape: a timed sequence the
  attention economy runs against. Programme length ≈ 18 cycles
  (the source's count), structured Shot A (cycles 1–9) / Shot B
  (cycles 10–18), the cut authored as the programme's midpoint
  event.
- **Gaze beat = timed lens answer window.** At the top of each
  cycle the programme opens a gaze window (the withdrawal's last
  ≈ 40%). An applause or compliment event fired inside the window
  scores as Redlight's existing **timed lens answer (+95)**; the
  same event outside the window scores its ordinary value
  (applause +70; compliment +35 immediate and floor +50, cap 500).
  This makes the source's phase-lock the literal skill of the
  rite: watch her face, answer when she looks at you.
- **Deep beat.** Reaching bottom dwell at depth ≥ 0.9 fires a
  deep-beat event in the applause class (+70) on the cycle's own
  cadence — capped by construction: at most one per cycle, and
  cycles are ≥ 20 steps apart, inside the pose cooldown shape
  (+55 / 30 steps) the economy already tolerates.
- **Attention decay.** The rite bleeds attention at the audience
  preset's decay (0.30 solo record / 0.38 / 0.46 live, per step,
  milli-units 0–1000) for its whole duration; decay never crosses
  the praise floor, as ever. The session arc the port-back
  identified — farm the floor early, spend it late — is the
  rite's arc too: Shot A is where compliments land cheap, Shot B
  is where the floor carries the close-up.
- **Reactive voice (torori).** One new event class, *gaze*, fired
  when a gaze window closes unanswered or is answered late. Per
  the reaction-set sizing law (PORT-BACK §1): **≥ 6 lines** for
  the class, uniform pick with last-line exclusion. Existing
  classes (applause, flash, compliment) fire unchanged during
  the rite. Line texts themselves are voice-production work, not
  specified here.
- **Revelation: Glimpsed → Known.** The cut is the revelation
  beat. In the solo-record preset the rite may be run as Shot B
  only (Glimpsed form: the face without the body's geometry);
  the full two-shot take is the Known form. The session-arc
  state moves on the full take, per the existing revelation rule.
- **The written record.** On rite end, the session record
  inscribes: cycles completed, deep beats, gaze windows answered
  / missed, and the shot split — in the existing session-stats
  shape. The body-writing archive stub is untouched by this rite.
- **Face states (estate machinery).** Two phase-keyed states,
  interpolated through intermediates, never swapped:
  *GAZE* (top of cycle: eyes open half-lidded, gaze to lens,
  emissive-iris layer ON — the switchable face layer, never a
  model property) and *DEEP* (bottom of cycle: lids slit/closed,
  head tilted down, cheek fullness ∝ depth). Neither is an
  arousal-engine peak state; the source has no culmination and
  none is synthesised.
